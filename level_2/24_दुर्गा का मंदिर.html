Coverage and translation notes

Coin terminology and amounts
The source alternates सावरेन, गिन्नियाँ and अशर्फियाँ for the gold coins. Its arithmetic values eight at 120 rupees and two at 30; those story amounts are retained without substituting a historical exchange rate.
Perception, testimony and source slips
Durga's changing expressions and words are narrated through Bhama's experience. Gorelal's later account follows the earlier overheard conversation without resolving the tension between them. ¶48 accidentally names Gorelal as the person mistaking distant carriages; the context clearly follows Brajnath's waiting, as rendered here. Every one of the 99 paragraph blocks is retained.
Final wordplay
The final ‘ears’ plays against earrings: she has acquired a permanent warning, expressed through the Hindi idiom कान होना. It is explained rather than replaced with an unrelated English joke.
