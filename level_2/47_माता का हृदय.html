¶1 माधवी की आँखों में सारा संसार अँधेरा हो रहा था। कोई अपना मददगार न दिखायी देता था। कहीं आशा की झलक न थी। उस निर्धन घर में वह अकेली पड़ी रोती थी और कोई आँसू पोंछनेवाला न था। उसके पति को मरे हुए 22 वर्ष हो गये थे। घर में कोई सम्पत्ति न थी। उसने न-जाने किन तकलीफों से अपने बच्चे को पाल-पोसकर बड़ा किया था। वही जवान बेटा आज उसकी गोद से छीन लिया गया था और छीननेवाले कौन थे ? Coverage and translation notes

Two maternal attachments
Madhavi's son Atmanand is imprisoned, not killed. The baby she tends is Bagchi's surviving youngest child. The translation keeps the two relationships distinct throughout.
A difficult printed phrase
In ¶20 तेज गाड़ी is unusual in a request concerning an infant. Carriage renders the apparent request for a child's carriage without altering the Hindi. The source's account of illness, remedies and recovery is translated as narrated.
The nominal sale in ¶33
Bagchi describes a symbolic transfer to a washerwoman intended, in his family's belief, to protect a child after earlier deaths. His speech itself distinguishes his intellectual disbelief from his emotional attachment to the practice.
Title
माता का हृदय means A Mother's Heart. The final paragraph makes compassion, rather than the revenge with which Madhavi began, the defining quality of that heart.
