¶1 In Banaras district lies a village called Bira. An old, childless Gond widow named Bhungi lived there. She possessed not a handspan of land, nor a home of her own. Coverage and translation notes

Narrative irony
The opening's supposed right to unpaid labour, the phrase ‘wicked thoughts’ in ¶7 and the description of the landlord as distinguished are bitterly ironic. They are retained rather than rewritten as straightforward approval.
Source and ending
Bhungi's claim about twelve years of cultivation is a character's argument, not legal advice. The opening says she owns no home, while later she speaks of generations in the hut; these refer to lack of ownership and longstanding occupancy. Lanka's burning is an epic comparison. The published deaths and destruction are translated without adding an aftermath.
