Coverage and translation notes

घर-जमाई
The title names a son-in-law who lives in his wife's parental household. Haridhan's lost property, work and expectations of family affection are central to that arrangement here.
The source's time references
The text says his mother died ten years ago when he was sixteen, while ¶16 calls him twenty-five and other passages count ten years in the in-laws' home. These approximate or uneven source references are preserved rather than reconciled.
Maternal imagery
The narrator's broad comparison of fathers and mothers in ¶13 is retained in full. Nandi is Shiva's bull; the comparison concerns care treated as optional. Mahabir in the ending refers to Hanuman, to whom Haridhan proposes offering sweets.
A difficult printed phrase
In ¶57 अधर से आम is irregular in this context. The English tentatively renders the action as picking mangoes overhead, since Haridhan is in the tree. The Hindi is copied unchanged.
