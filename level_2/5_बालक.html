Coverage and translation notes

Narrator and prejudice
Caste claims, derogatory labels for Gomti and the narrator's contempt for poorer people are retained as his perspective. The ending exposes the gap between his respectable self-image and Gangu's generosity.
Illness and parenthood
The claim of spirit possession in ¶26 is Gangu's description, not a medical diagnosis. The six-month interval is the narrator's insinuation about biological paternity; Gangu explicitly accepts the child as his own. Nanda is Krishna's foster father. The source separates ¶11–12 and ¶13–14 mid-speech; those paragraph boundaries remain intact.
