Coverage and translation notes

Anna and the title
Anna is the child's affectionate name for his nurse, Kailasi. Dhanvantari is the divine physician invoked by Indramani. महातीर्थ means a supremely sacred place or pilgrimage; the ending makes compassionate care the greatest pilgrimage.
The toy animals in ¶24
Although the dog and cat are initially described as if alive, the paragraph explicitly calls these things lifeless and includes a wingless bird. The translation preserves that unfolding description.
Uneven time references
The source mentions three weeks in ¶25 but returns to two weeks in ¶48 before Indramani describes three weeks in ¶53. These source time references are preserved.
