Satire
The theological and scriptural claims are the characters' self-serving arguments. The story satirizes gluttony and sophistry; the translation retains the crowd's praise and the final quarrel.
Sweets and speech
The regional sweet names and their city associations are retained exactly as the story assigns them, without substituting modern culinary claims. The wife's rural Hindi is translated in informal English. Chintamani's final mat-throwing and the intervention complete the published ending.
