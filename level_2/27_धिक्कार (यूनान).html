Narrative judgements
The narrator explicitly treats the repentance as a deceitful performance and praises the mother's choice. Those severe judgements, the crowd's cruelty and the final imprisonment are all retained as literary content. The story ends with the narrator's tribute; it does not narrate a subsequent battle or the prisoner's death.
