Siddheshwar is the deceased eldest brother, Rameshwar the father of Jageshwar, and Vishweshwar the younger uncle. Tapeshwari is Siddheshwar's daughter. English ‘brothers’ in ¶75 follows the affectionate Hindi for cousins. Bigha is a regional land measure whose exact area is not specified.
Source phrasing
The mixed Chaubey–Dubey saying in ¶45 is retained and means the attempt at gain ends in ruin. ‘Strip you bare’ in ¶32 interprets the unusual खड़ा कर from its threatening context. Assertions about hereditary rights are characters' positions, not independently stated law. The final line is the story's full ending.
