Coverage and translation notes

Conflict retained
The narration presents both the family's suffering under the boycott penalty and Amba's final acceptance of sacrifice. Neither the coercive exchanges nor the justification for collective discipline has been removed.
Money and names
Chhakauri is the shortened form of Chhakaurilal; Amba is addressed affectionately as Ambe. The 101-rupee penalty and proposed 25-rupee monthly employment are retained. The published धोले in ¶14 is understood as the tiny coin धेले from the repeated sales context; no additional transaction is invented. The story ends as he goes to the moneylender, before any loan is shown.
