Coverage and translation notes

Moral and religious argument
The employer's claim to save jobs is unverified within the story. Dinanath knowingly falsifies a page and later struggles with fear; the translation preserves both his responsibility and the pressure exerted upon him. His final theology remains his argument, not the translator's conclusion.
Illness and ending
The narrator attributes the ordinary malaria's terrifying mental effects to entrenched fear. The final poison threat is retained as dialogue; no act of poisoning occurs. Gauri's frightened silence is the complete published ending.
