Coverage and translation notes

Source inconsistencies
The published ¶25 says सहयोगियों where its surrounding argument suggests opponents of cooperation; the literal ‘cooperationists’ is retained rather than silently corrected. ¶8 names more servants earlier but then says four began drinking; that count is retained.
Festival and politics
Phag, chautal and the bawdy Holi verses are song forms in this setting; the latter are not works attributed to the saint Kabir. Vibhishana invokes a culturally familiar image of someone who changes sides. The odd printed separators around the interruption in ¶31 are represented as speaker changes, preserving every spoken contribution.
