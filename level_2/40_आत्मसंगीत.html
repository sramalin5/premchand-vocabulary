¶24 Suddenly Manorama sprang up in a trance. She felt that the music had come closer. Its beauty and joy had grown more intense, as a lamp grows brighter when its wick is raised. Before it had enchanted; now it stirred the soul to ecstasy. Distraught, Manorama said, ‘Ah! Why won't you say yourself what you want? Ah, what a melody of renunciation, how utterly overwhelming! I cannot be patient any longer. As water longs to flow downhill, as breath yearns for air, as fragrance longs to take flight, so I long for this heavenly music.
