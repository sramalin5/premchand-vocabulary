Coverage and translation notes

Intoxication
The title refers primarily to the intoxicating effect of borrowed privilege. The narrator admits his earlier egalitarianism was based on circumstance, and the train episode tests it in action. Cannabis consumption by the Thakur is a separate literal intoxication.
Historical travel and wording
Intermediate class was a railway category between second and third. The ruler of Changli is named as in the source; no identity is supplied from elsewhere. The final English sentence and its Hindi parenthetical translation express the same rebuke and are represented once in English. The rural phrasing in ¶53 is kept colloquial.
