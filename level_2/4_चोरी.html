Coverage and translation notes

Irony and uncertainty
Father's ‘medicine’ and his fellow ‘patients’ are rendered in the child's deliberately naive terms; the surrounding description suggests drinking alcohol. The confused pronouns in ¶5, describing Uncle's simplicity and anger, are retained without assigning an invented second person.
Money and food
Sixteen annas made a rupee; the story's two-pice purchase leaves fifteen and a half annas. Seer and maund are historical weight measures. Khirmohan, laddus and the other foods retain their distinct identities. Aunt's apparent accusations in ¶32 are bitterly sarcastic: status, rather than truth, determines whose account is believed.
