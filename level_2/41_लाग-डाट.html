Coverage and translation notes

The rival systems
Swaraj is self-rule; swadeshi favours locally produced goods. The Sanatani and Arya Samaj labels distinguish religious affiliations, while Ayurveda and Unani name different medical traditions. These are the characters' positions in the story.
Uncertain printed wording
In ¶9 साल बनाते हैं is unclear in the published text; affect fashionable airs follows the surrounding criticism of fashionable habits and is tentative. In ¶38 स्वयंसेवा literally suggests self-service, but the speech condemns selfish advantage, so self-seeking is used. The Hindi is unchanged.
Title
लाग-डाट evokes rivalry, antagonism and competitive contention. The ending turns that rivalry into the stimulus for mutual reform.
