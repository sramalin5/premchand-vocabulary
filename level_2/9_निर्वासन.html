Coverage and translation notes

Ending and missing explanation
The published story ends with Maryada's departure. It does not describe how her proposed stratagem enabled her escape; no account has been invented to fill this gap.
Social prejudice and names
Basu is the familiar form of Vasudev. The sister-in-law's relationship to Parashuram is conveyed by her address, without inventing a name. The characters' sexist, caste-based and religious prejudices remain their statements, including the contradictions in Parashuram's claimed rejection of untouchability. Maryada's final childlessness is figurative loss, not a medical claim.
