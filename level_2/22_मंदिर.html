Her life departed; she gave her life for her child. Mother, blessed are you! Such devotion, reverence and faith as yours are rare even among the gods.

Coverage and translation notes

Source slips
The website contains several typographical or damaged forms, including अलम for the child's role as her support, चुड़ैलों का अव and बालक की मंदिर in ¶21. The Hindi is unchanged; the English gives the locally evident sense without adding a missing event. The two regional forms of the evil-eye word also remain in Hindi.
Belief and narrative judgement
Dreams, charms and ritual promises are characters' beliefs. The priest's assurances are not medical guidance. His alleged compassion and the devotees' claims to purity are presented with the story's irony intact. The final assault, the child's death and Sukhiya's death are all retained.
