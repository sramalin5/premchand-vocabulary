Coverage and translation notes

Rebirth within the story
The explanation linking Jawahir with Shiv Tahal is the holy man's account and drives Ram Tahal's transformation. The horoscope and supernatural explanation are retained as narrative elements, not verified claims.
Ambiguities and timing
The source gives Jawahir's age as two and a half before the first visit, then says three further years bring his sixth year; those statements are preserved without adjusting the chronology. बाँधा in ¶13 is read from context as a matching carriage partner. The holy man says ‘all your property’ in ¶37 even though the earlier account describes secret sales of produce; that stronger statement is retained.
