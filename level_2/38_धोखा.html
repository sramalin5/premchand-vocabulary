Coverage and translation notes

Refrain and musical setting
Every repeated source block of the song is translated separately. Madhuban is retained as the song's place-name and devotional image. वीणा in Prabha's wish and सितार in the performance remain different instruments.
Portraits and perspective
The source repeats a gallery division description and names historical and religious figures in its own order; that order is retained. Prabha's feelings of sin and duty remain her perspective. The ending retains her question about the spiritual cost of the deception rather than substituting an uncomplicated happy ending.
