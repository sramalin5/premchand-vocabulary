Coverage and translation notes

The source's displaced sequence
Paragraphs 36–39 return to the landlord's hundred-rupee demand and the loss of the fields after ¶35 has already narrated Girdhari's disappearance. This discontinuous published order is preserved exactly; the passage has not been moved or filled in.
The appearances at the end
The final events are narrated as apparitions and a haunting, including Kalikadin's account of the well. No recovery, discovery of a body or additional explanation has been invented.
A printed word in ¶9
शरीर means body, but the contrast with village sympathy appears to require शहर, city. City is a tentative contextual rendering; the original word is unchanged.
The title and the fields
बलिदान means sacrifice. The story connects duty to the father, the work invested in land, and the ruin caused by unequal economic power. Field names are kept as proper names rather than given invented explanations.
