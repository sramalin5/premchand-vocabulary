Coverage and translation notes

Title and inner vision
झांकी means a glimpse or tableau. The external temple display leads into the narrator's inward vision of Krishna, family affection and reconciliation.
Two printed names
The source calls the temple's merchant घूरेमल in ¶29 and घूरेलाल in ¶35. The English retains Ghuremal and Ghurelal respectively, with this note rather than silently altering the Hindi.
Festivals and the ending
The dispute concerns Teej gifts, while the city is celebrating Janmashtami, Krishna's birth festival. The story ends with Jaydev's question; it does not narrate the narrator's return home or an actual reconciliation there.
Music and memories
The musical instruments and vocal form are retained as cultural terms. His brother's conduct is recalled as hurtful, but the music changes his emotional response; the earlier events are not erased.
