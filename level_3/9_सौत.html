Coverage and translation notes

Names and printed errors
The source alternates दासी, दसिया and दस्सो, and once uses राजो for Raziya. English uses Dasi or Dasiya in the corresponding passages, and Raziya consistently. Obvious printed slips remain unchanged in the Hindi.
Source paragraph break
Paragraph 51 breaks after यहां, with the sentence continuing in ¶52. The English preserves that division with a connecting dash rather than merging or omitting a paragraph.
Jokhu and the worker
The name जोखूं appears for the Chaudhri's son in ¶54 and for the younger boy later. The source does not clarify the repetition; no separate invented identity has been added. मंजूर in ¶56 is interpreted contextually as the worker, since he has been left in charge of Raziya's house.
The closing claim
When Raziya says Dasiya's man is still alive, she figuratively takes on the role of protector and provider. The source has already explicitly narrated Ramu's death; the closing statement is not a reversal of that event.
