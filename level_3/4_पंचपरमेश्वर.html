Coverage and translation notes

The title and the councils
पंचपरमेश्वर presents the impartial councillor as a seat of divine justice. Panchayat means the village council or its arbitration hearing; panch is a councillor, and sarpanch its presiding member. The repeated closing cry is rendered Glory to God in the councillor.
Allegation versus fact
Alagu suspects poisoning after the first bullock's death, but the source does not establish it. The second bullock's death is directly narrated as the consequence of overwork and neglect.
Irony and violence
The household-official comparison in ¶6 and the argument-filled stick in ¶43 are ironic. The latter retains the narrator's reference to Alagu's use of a stick rather than replacing it with a peaceful invented exchange.
The aunt's timeline
The source says the transfer occurred three years earlier while her plea also speaks of passing a year in distress. Both details are retained.
