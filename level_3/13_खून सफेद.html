Coverage and translation notes

The title's idiom
खून सफेद refers to the loss of natural affection and loyalty, not literal blood colour. The closing sentence applies it to the parents' failure to welcome their son without caste restrictions.
Sadho's disappearance
Devaki initially suspects a spell. The grown Sadho later explains that he followed the minister voluntarily and initially refused advice to return. The translation keeps belief and later explanation distinct.
पादरी in the return scene
The elders ask whether he too became a padre, and Sadho agrees. The source does not give further detail about ordination or his formal position; none is added.
Food and family terms
दादा in the young child's household speech refers here to Father. Lallu is his affectionate family name. The changing famine and harvest scenes, including the chronological seasonal references, remain as printed.
