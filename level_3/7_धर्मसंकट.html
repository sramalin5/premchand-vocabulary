Coverage and translation notes

The title's dilemma
धर्मसंकट means a conflict of duties or moral dilemma. The ending places Rupchand's own defence against the promise he believes he owes Kamini's honour.
Private letters and public testimony
The two letters are translated separately and completely. Kamini's later sworn denial contradicts the earlier narrative; that contradiction is the plot, not a source gap to repair.
Silent exchange in ¶37
The appeals and promises are conveyed through eyes and inward understanding. They are not audible statements made in court.
Narrative judgments
The story's broad claims about men, women and cultural reform belong to its narrator and historical viewpoint. They are retained rather than rewritten. The text does not narrate a detailed charge sheet; no additional legal facts have been invented.
