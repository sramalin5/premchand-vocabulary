Coverage and translation notes

Narrative voice
The narrator’s irony and the attendant’s accusations are retained as spoken perspectives. The text’s moral judgements about marriage and appetite are not added conclusions. Names are Rafaqat Husain throughout.
Source wording
All 21 paragraphs are included. In ¶6 a visibly damaged जल òोत is interpreted as a water-source or spring from its context; the Hindi is not repaired. In ¶19 महनामथ is an unusual form, rendered contextually as uproar. The metaphorical water vessel in ¶10 describes his inability to preserve resources, not a newly invented event.
