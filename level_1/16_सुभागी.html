Coverage and translation notes

Complete source
All 89 prose paragraphs are retained. दादा is rendered Father in Subhagi’s address to Tulsi, while her later respectful paternal address to Sajan Singh is also retained. The source once calls Tulsi चौधरी; this title is preserved.
Social setting and numbers
The opening widowhood at eleven, the community’s acceptance of remarriage and the eventual crossing of caste restrictions remain explicit. The source gives a five-hundred-rupee debt, three years and fifteen-rupee monthly payments; those figures are not silently reconciled. Funeral expenditure and the family’s attitudes remain the characters’ choices, not added advice.
