Coverage and translation notes

Voice and politics
All 40 prose paragraphs are preserved. Names are Qadir and Maiku. The Mahatma remains the speaker’s designation; no outside historical episode is inserted. Claims about the police are the characters’ speech. The crowd is first called thousands by the narrator, while Maiku estimates hundreds; both figures are retained.
Violence and irony
The volunteer explicitly disapproves of the destruction. Maiku’s remorse, his violent turn against the shop and his threat at the end are all translated, without converting the story into a simple nonviolent moral.
