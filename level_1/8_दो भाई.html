Coverage and translation notes

Numbers and relationships
Five score, four score and similar counts preserve the source’s counting by twenties. Madhav, Maddhu and Lalu are the forms used for the younger brother; Kedar, Champa, Shyama and Kalavati remain consistent.
Source ambiguity
The botanical comparison in ¶5 is compressed: it mentions offspring for both, one pale fruit on one tree, then Kedar’s desire for children. Its wording is translated without inventing a family detail. The irony of the brothers becoming ‘wise’ in ¶8–9 is retained. All 43 prose paragraphs and the child’s verse are included.
