Coverage and translation notes

Judgement and revelation
All 111 prose paragraphs are translated. The assumptions about prostitution, respectability, youth and women belong to the onlookers, and are contradicted or unsettled by the ending. They are not endorsed as factual descriptions of the sleeping woman.
Names and uncertainty
The source calls the runner बसंत and later names the husband वसंतलाल; the translation retains Basant and Vasantlal rather than silently changing either name. The narrative strongly suggests a connection but does not explicitly identify every earlier onlooker in the final dialogue. The bench is described with बिल्लौर, retained as crystalline.
Historical products
Okasa, Chandrodaya and monkey-gland treatment are retained as the characters’ period references, not health recommendations. The last exchange concerns a bride whose gauna has not yet taken place; the custom is explained in the glossary without supplying an outside family history.
