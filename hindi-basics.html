Consonants · व्यंजन

Consonants are demonstrated with their inherent a sound: क = ka. A halant (्) removes that vowel: क् = k. In words, the inherent vowel is sometimes dropped. h in kh, gh, th, and similar combinations indicates a puff of breath. The dots in ṭ, ḍ, and ṇ mark sounds made with the tongue curled back.

Additional sounds & signs

The dot below a consonant is called a nukta. It distinguishes additional sounds used in Hindi and borrowed vocabulary.
