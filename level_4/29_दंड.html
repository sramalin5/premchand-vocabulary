Coverage and translation notes

Title and social punishment
दंड means punishment. The story juxtaposes corruption, a coercive fast and a social boycott whose consequences reach an innocent daughter.
Claims and perspectives
The account preserves the characters' claims about religious merit, Brahmin-killing and ghosts without treating them as established explanations. The narrator's generalizations about beauty and marriage are retained as written.
A name and a nickname
The official is Mr. G. Sinha. Triveni is his daughter; Tibbo at the end is apparently an affectionate form of address for her. The source does not explicitly explain the nickname.
Traditional terms
A bigha is a traditional unit of land. The reference to overseas transportation invokes a severe penal exile; the printed comparison is retained without adding a legal ruling.
