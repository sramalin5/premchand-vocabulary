Coverage and translation notes

Source paragraph boundaries
Several sentences are split between published paragraphs. Connecting dashes retain those boundaries without omitting text.
Names and numbers
Sona is first proposed as Sonaram, later assigned Mohansarup Sukul; these stages of the disguise are retained. Mangru appears with minor spelling variation in Hindi and is rendered consistently.
Ritual and comic claims
The quoted devotional lines are translated as printed, without correcting them from other versions. Exaggerated eating claims, purity rules and the comparison of three wives with three afflictions or humors belong to the satire.
An incomplete invocation
Paragraph 175 begins a broken invocation involving Yashoda, Keshava and Murari. Its printed fragments are retained in English rather than completed from memory.
