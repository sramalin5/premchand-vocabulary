Coverage and translation notes

Chess terms and the final irony
किश्त and शह are rendered check. फरजी and वजीर name the minister piece in the historical game; farzi is retained at the disputed move. The king's capture outside and the players' concern for their board kings remain distinct.
The military visitor
The conversation with Mir's wife in ¶46–47 reveals the summons as her stratagem. The translation preserves the delayed reveal and adds no unseen military order.
The wife's saying in ¶18
रानी रूठेंगी, अपना सुहाग लेंगी is a dismissive saying: let an offended person withdraw what is theirs; they have no claim over us. Its queen-and-married-fortune wording is retained rather than replaced with an invented equivalent.
History as narrated
The text's account of Wajid Ali Shah's capture and the city's bloodless surrender is translated as the story presents it. No additional historical events, dates or scenes from adaptations are inserted.
