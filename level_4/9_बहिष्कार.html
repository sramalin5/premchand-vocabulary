Coverage and translation notes

Title and social setting
बहिष्कार means ostracism or boycott. The story depicts caste prejudice; references to supposedly high and low birth express that social hierarchy, not an endorsement of it.
An undisclosed family history
Somdatt never states the details of Govindi's parentage or her mother's circumstances. The translation preserves that uncertainty.
The train death
The account says Gyanchandra was crushed beneath a train. It does not explicitly describe how he came to be on the track.
Cultural expressions
Twice-born refers to traditional caste status associated with initiation. The invocation of the Ganges in paragraph 8 implies death in the river, without spelling it out.
