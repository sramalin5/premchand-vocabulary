Coverage and translation notes

Title and narrator
शांति means peace. The first-person narrator is addressed as Shyama in paragraph 49; her husband is called Babuji and is not otherwise named.
The speakers' judgments
Claims about gender, civilization, literature and duty belong to the narrator and her husband. Their changing views are translated as expressed, without adding agreement or correcting their ideology.
A name variation
The source calls one family Bagri in paragraph 25 and uses Bagchi in paragraph 32. These printed forms are retained rather than silently harmonized.
Literary and religious names
Oscar Wilde, Carlyle, Ruskin and Emerson follow the source's transliterated names. The Ramayana, Bhaktamal, Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads and Mahabharata are retained as named works; no outside passages have been added.
