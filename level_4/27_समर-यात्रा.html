Coverage and translation notes

The title
समर-यात्रा suggests a march into battle. The story explicitly makes its battle one of truth and nonviolent resistance.
Statements within the speech
The claims about salt supplies, taxation and national character are the leader's speech as printed. No separate historical verification or outside text is added.
Song and a difficult saying
Every printed line and repetition of the song is retained separately. The coarse rice-water saying in paragraph 42 disparages accepting a paltry benefit from servitude; its image remains in English.
Names and forms of address
Kodai, Nohri, Maiku, Ganga, Sevaram, Bhajan Singh, Ghure and Kale Khan are spelled consistently. Aunt and sister-in-law are social forms of address, not newly inferred family relationships.
