Coverage and translation notes

Opening contradiction
The printed opening says अभिन्नता, 'sameness,' though the descriptions and later विषमताओं emphasize differences. The unusual word is retained rather than silently corrected.
Friendship and the judgment
Naim agrees to accept one minute of private conversation instead of the decree payment. No hidden cash payment is invented. The final exchange plays on this arrangement and on reciprocal humiliation.
Terms
A lakh is 100,000. The Court of Wards administered estates under legal guardianship. Ekadashi is associated with fasting; here the reference describes the whole household going without food. The first paragraph's descriptions are followed consistently in assigning Naim and Kailas their roles.
