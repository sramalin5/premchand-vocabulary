Coverage and translation notes

Beauty and conduct
The final line means Suresh's way of seeing has changed, although Mangala's appearance has not. Earlier generalizations about women, Europe, and physical beauty remain the narrator's or characters' views.
Chronology and wording
The source first places Vimal in Rangoon for three years, later says he has been gone two years, and later again says three. These inconsistencies are preserved. पुण्य-माला is rendered 'garland of merit'; its strange presence among death's disguises is not silently changed.
Names and symbols
Shitala, Vimal Singh, Suresh Singh, and Mangala are used consistently. Vermilion symbolizes married status; Lakshmi symbolizes the home's fortune. The Mahabharata comparison means a tremendous household battle. Rupee amounts and traditional seer measures are retained.
