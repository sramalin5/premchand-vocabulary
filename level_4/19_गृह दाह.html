Title
गृह दाह suggests a home consumed by fire; the destruction here is primarily the household's emotional ruin.
Printed numbers and chronology
The source prints ‘660-70’ in paragraph 108, apparently a duplicated digit; the English reads sixty to seventy rupees. The story also moves from a five-year interval to a reference to seven years; those stated intervals are retained.
The final illness
The explanation of Devprakash's illness is Gyanprakash's account as printed. The translation does not add a medical diagnosis or endorse the stated mechanism.
A literal image
Paragraph 75 prints ‘लहर’—wave—for what steadies life. That image is retained rather than silently replacing the source word.
