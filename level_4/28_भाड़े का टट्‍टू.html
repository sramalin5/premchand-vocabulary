Coverage and translation notes

Title and irony
The hired pony means a mercenary servant of payment. The final accusation reverses the friends' opening exchange.
Difficult printed wording
Paragraph 19 prints ‘हाकिम’ even though the ensuing account makes Ramesh a public critic; ‘ruler’ retains the printed word. Paragraph 46's ‘दोनों के उपकार’ is awkward; its immediate context concerns assistance and reform for the disadvantaged.
Evidence and later conduct
The narrator states Ramesh's innocence in the first prosecution, then describes his later robberies. Those two stages remain distinct, as do his wife's later assertion of a false charge and the narrator's account.
The financial calculation
The stated daily fee, reward and estimated totals are retained, even where the character's rough arithmetic does not align exactly.
