Coverage and translation notes

Child speech
Bhan's three slogans in paragraph 12 are printed in lisping child pronunciation. The English gives their apparent sense: the flag, self-rule in prison and opposition to toddy and liquor.
Forms of address
Mridula's ‘बाबू जी’ in the account of the court and procession refers to her husband, later named Hridayesh. ‘अम्माँ’ is his mother, whom Mridula also calls Mother.
Testimony within the story
The account of revenue collection and the shootings is Mridula's narration, retained in full. No external historical details have been added.
A traditional weight
A maund is a traditional bulk weight; the stated price of one and three-quarter rupees is rendered as one rupee and twelve annas.
