Coverage and translation notes

The plant and the title
कामना-तरु means the tree of longing or desire. Chanda first plants the mango seedling; the prince later tends it with her. It carries their shared memory through the separation.
Source paragraph breaks
Sentences are split across ¶10–11, ¶15–16, ¶17–19, ¶31–32 and ¶39–40. The English preserves these numbered boundaries with continuing quotations or dashes rather than merging paragraphs.
Time and belief
The prince is captive twenty years; the farmer says Chanda waited ten years before her death. Her identity as the bird emerges through a dream, his perception and village belief. The translation retains that progression.
The final repairs
The prince begins rebuilding the walls. The ending attributes the completed roof, walls and flower beds to the village farmers' care after his death; no extra scene of that work is invented.
