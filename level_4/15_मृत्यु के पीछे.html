Coverage and translation notes

Title
मृत्यु के पीछे means ‘After Death’ here: the story concerns recognition and a change of heart after Ishwarchandra's death.
Printed irregularities
The source's ‘नाली’ at the end of paragraph 1 is rendered as ‘channel’ without substituting a different image. Paragraph 14 has a damaged form for a mountain stream, and paragraph 18 has the printed ‘पुनुरुद्धार’; the exact Hindi remains untouched.
Conflicting judgments
The accusations about law and journalism are the speakers' arguments, not independent claims about those professions.
