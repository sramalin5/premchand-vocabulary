Coverage and translation notes

Belief versus the narrator's revelation
Paragraph 8 explicitly reveals tonic rather than poison. Jeevandas continues to think he has killed his wife and child; later references to sacrifice and murder preserve his mistaken understanding, not a second undisclosed death.
The title and reversals
प्रारब्ध is destiny, particularly consequences unfolding beyond one's control. His beliefs about fate and divine power reverse several times; the translation keeps those changing positions distinct.
Names and the school
Lakhandas is also affectionately called Lakkhu. The school's name is a separate source paragraph and remains separately numbered.
The ending
The source ends with Jeevandas's death in the river, arising from fear and remorse. No further family response or philosophical resolution is supplied. This is a translation of the tragic literary ending, not a claim that his final conclusion is sound.
