Coverage and translation notes

Atonement and the title
मुक्ति-मार्ग means the path to release or liberation. The imposed pilgrimage ruins Buddhu, while the ending finds release from rivalry in shared labour, food and unadorned confession.
Evidence and later confession
The fire is first treated as an open secret without proof. Its responsibility becomes explicit only when Buddhu admits it at the end. Harihar's false testimony about the heifer is likewise exposed by the final confession.
The interest calculation
The source names two rupees interest and says it would equal the principal in eight months, without fully specifying the rate's units. The translation retains both statements without inventing a loan schedule.
The last meal
Buddhu still observes Jhingur's restriction against food cooked by him, so shaping and baking are divided. Their reconciliation is not rewritten as the complete disappearance of every social custom.
