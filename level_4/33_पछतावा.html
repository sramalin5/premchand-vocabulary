Coverage and translation notes

Irony
Descriptions of the landlord's welfare measures and the 'adornments' of landlordship are ironic. The violence and financial manipulation remain explicit.
Source wording
The final statement offers everything to Durganath; the text ends without reporting his arrival or the estate's later fate. None is supplied. The malformed printed title of Vishnu's thousand-name hymn is translated by its identifiable sense.
Units and institutions
A bigha is a traditional land area; Chait is a spring lunar month; cowries were small monetary units. The Court of Wards was an institution administering estates under legal guardianship. Turmeric and jaggery here imply treatment after a beating.
