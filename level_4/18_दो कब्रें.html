Broken source paragraphs
Several sentences are split by the published paragraph boundaries. Connecting dashes preserve those boundaries while allowing the English sentence to continue; no passage is omitted.
Social labels
The story examines prejudice toward a courtesan's daughter and the limits of professed liberalism. Derogatory descriptions are the characters' or narrator's wording, retained in context.
An opening textual difficulty
The printed ‘वीरांगनाओं’ in paragraph 1 literally means heroic women, but the surrounding description concerns courtesans. The English follows that context; the Hindi is unchanged.
An unstated cause of death
The narrative jumps from Sulochana's distress to her grave. It does not state the immediate manner or cause of her death, and the translation adds none.
