Coverage and translation notes

The title and movement
The title means ‘The Liquor Shop.’ Picketing, swadeshi goods and khadi appear as activities of the national movement in the story.
The characters' arguments
Claims about English ancestors, inherited virtue and gender belong to the speakers. Their wording is translated without treating them as independent historical findings.
The committee's numbers
The opening specifies twenty-five members; Jayaram later says thirty. Both source numbers are retained.
A remedy and a rebuke
The lemonade seller's turmeric-and-jaggery remark implies treatment for injuries. ‘Pull my ears’ is Jayaram's offer to accept a reprimand if he fails to stop sales.
