Coverage and translation notes

Character descriptions
The source's second paragraph assigns the names after contrasting the two men in an awkward backward reference. The translation follows the subsequent explicit comparison of Dharmadas's beauty and Khazanchand's wealth.
Religious rhetoric
The preacher's promises, the captors' threats, and the characters' competing religious claims are retained as statements within the story. They are not claims about present-day communities.
Numbers and ending
A lakh is 100,000. The text initially has five riders; after one is shot, four remain. Dharmadas's death is discovered without an explicit account of its cause, so none is added.
