Coverage and translation notes

Title and sentence fragments
Damul refers to penal transportation. The printed title uses दामुल, while the narrative also prints डामुल. Several paragraphs split sentences; they remain separate, with connecting dashes in English.
Chronology and belief
The court gives fourteen years, but the narrative repeatedly describes fifteen years away. Both are preserved. Gopinath's rebirth as Krishnachandra is the characters' belief and the narrative's spiritual interpretation, not an added factual explanation.
Pramila's response
Her final rejection of the claim that every event serves human welfare is preserved fully, alongside her husband's continuing belief. The two responses are not reconciled by omission.
Measures and wording
A lakh is 100,000. Two annas per rupee means an eighth of the price. Christmas is बड़ा दिन. The unusual phrase about workers' women in the final festival paragraph is rendered 'hard-pressed women'; the context does not establish a different occupation.
