Coverage and translation notes

History and narration
The final claim that traditions are more trustworthy than history is the narrator's assertion, translated as printed. The edition does not independently certify this episode as historical fact.
State and ruler
The title distinguishes devotion to the state from personal obedience to the monarch. The Raja's preference for native rule and his refusal of office are both preserved.
Names omitted by the source
The source prints an incomplete English name as मि... in several places. English retains Mr. ... rather than inventing a name.
