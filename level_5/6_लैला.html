Coverage and translation notes

Verse
The Persian lines are translated from the wording printed in this source, without importing a different edition. Their transliteration is imperfect, so the second couplet's wording is approximate.
Unusual wording
क्षुधा in the description of her music literally means hunger and is retained. The chess phrase about spring is likewise translated literally because the intended idiom is uncertain. Farzi is kept as a chess-piece name rather than silently assuming modern rules.
Political viewpoints
The changing judgments about monarchy, popular rule, and the people belong to the narrative and characters. Laila's final letter states her interpretation of Nadir's silence; it does not erase the earlier account of his conflict.
Measures and allusions
A crore is ten million. Jamshid and Naushirvan evoke legendary Persian kingship and justice. The lovers' travels and the source's five-year reference are preserved without adding a historical chronology.
