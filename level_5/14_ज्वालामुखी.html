Coverage and translation notes

Who is “सरकार”?
The respectful term सरकार does not by itself identify the employer’s gender. The early dialogue therefore uses “employer”; the narrator discovers the woman in ¶9. The place of departure is omitted in the source’s dialogue in ¶6 and remains an ellipsis.
Dream and allegory
The source never supplies a personal name or a definitive identity for the employer. Its ending leaves the narrated experience poised between dream and moral revelation; no explanation is added.
Source paragraph boundaries
The quoted letter begun in ¶20 continues in ¶21, and the narrator’s thought in ¶38 continues in ¶39. These paragraph divisions are preserved. The source’s बिहार in ¶44 is read in context as विहार, a place of recreation, rather than the region Bihar.
Terms and references
The Mutiny refers to the historical uprising mentioned as गदर. Sheikh Chilli evokes fanciful, impractical schemes. In ¶21, डिगरी is a legal decree obtained on a claim, distinct from the academic degrees mentioned earlier.
