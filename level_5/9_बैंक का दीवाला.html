Coverage and translation notes

Printed names and uncertainty
The source prints ताजा-स्टील, retained as Taja Steel rather than silently substituting another company name. Its awkward line about equality during Shivdas's encounter is kept close to the wording rather than reversed.
Responsibility and numbers
The narrative contrasts a legal right to refuse debts with Jagdish's moral decision. Several income and allowance figures shift during his thinking; their stated monthly or annual units are retained rather than reconciled.
Financial terms and measures
A lakh is 100,000. Nine rupees per hundred is nine percent. Promissory notes and time-expired claims are rendered in context; the story is not a present-day legal or investment guide.
Names and allusions
Lalla and Lal Sahib refer to the son. Din Singh and Poverty-town are his father's playful explanation. The references to Rama, Bhishma, and Rana Pratap express the ideal of sacrifice within Jagdish's reflection.
