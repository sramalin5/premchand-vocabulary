Coverage and translation notes

Forms of address and names
Rampyari is often called Pyari; Ramdulari is often Dulari. दादा here addresses the elderly father-in-law. “Mistress” translates स्वामिनी or मालकिन as the woman responsible for the household.
Regional vocabulary
The farming vocabulary includes हार, the village fields; मोट, a leather water-lifting bag; and उख, sugarcane. उंचवा in ¶73 is understood as the upland field designation. सगाई in the discussions of Pyari and Jokhu refers to entering a marriage, rather than merely a modern engagement ceremony.
Figures and source details
Jokhu’s claims about eating a seer and a quarter per meal, two seers for two meals, and the later half-seer are retained without arithmetic correction. A seer and a bigha are customary weight and land units whose exact local values are not specified.
The ending
The final exchange suggests growing intimacy, but the source ends with Pyari’s response. No subsequent marriage or other outcome is supplied.
